Research

My research is focused on studying strongly correlated electrons using numerical methods.

Systems of strongly correlated electrons are those in which the interactions between the electrons are so strong that they cannot be treated as independent particles. They are "correlated", meaning that the behavior of one electron is strongly influenced by the behavior of the others. This kind of behavior arises in certain materials, called strongly correlated materials, and results in a variety of interesting phenomena, such as high-temperature superconductivity and magnetism, which have potential technological applications.

In order to harness these materials, we need to understand the mechanisms that give rise to these phenomena. Systems of strongly correlated electrons are described by quantum mechanics, which provides equations that predict the behavior of the electrons. However, these equations are too complex to be solved exactly. In order to get quantitative results, we resort to numerical methods that allow us to find approximate solutions to these equations. My research project is to develop and apply such numerical methods to study strongly correlated electrons.
